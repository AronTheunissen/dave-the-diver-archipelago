## 💡 TIPS FOR ANALYSIS

1. **Watch a 100% playthrough** on YouTube to see everything
2. **Check the wiki:** https://dave-the-diver.fandom.com/
3. **Focus on progression first** - what's absolutely required?
4. **Don't forget hidden content** - secret areas, special events
5. **Consider difficulty tiers** - some items unlock in tiers
6. **Recipe ingredients matter** - if a recipe needs a rare fish, that fish becomes important
