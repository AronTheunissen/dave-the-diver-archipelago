# Sushi Dish Max Upgrade Levels

Fill in the **Max Level** column by researching each dish in-game.
In Bancho Sushi, open the dish research menu and check the max star level for each sushi.

Dishes with max level 1 have no upgrades and should NOT be added to `dish_upgrade_locations`.
Dishes with max level 2+ should be added.

## How to find max level
Open the dish research screen in the restaurant and check the max number of upgrade stars shown for each dish.

## Notes

- Once max levels are filled in, add sushi dishes to `dish_upgrade_locations` in `locations.py`
- The new option should be called something like `sushi_upgrade_checks` with values: `none`, `first_3_levels`, `first_5_levels`, `all_levels`
- The existing `dish_upgrades` option controls cooked dishes (8051xxx); sushi could be a separate toggle
- DREDGE aberration sushi should use `dlc_dredge` category tag
